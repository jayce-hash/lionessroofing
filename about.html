About Us

At Lioness Roofing, we believe roofing is more than just shingles and nails - it's about protecting families, honoring trust, and doing the right thing even when no one is watching.

We are a veteran-owned, local, family-rooted company with a heart for service. With nearly a decade of hands-on experience in construction and a genuine passion for roofing, we have firsthand knowledge of how the small details make the biggest difference. No two roofs are the same - even if they look it from the outside - and I treat every project as a unique opportunity to protect what matters most: people's homes, families, and peace of mind.

Who We Work For.

Talon Brown is the proud owner of Lioness Roofing, a trusted roofing and construction company based in Powder Springs, Georgia. With nearly a decade of hands on industry experience, Talon has built his business on a foundation of honesty, integrity, and service to others over profit - values he lives by both on and off the job site.

He previously stepped away from a large nationwide roofing company - not because he couldn't succeed there, but because he wanted to build something better. Something personal. Something that allowed him to serve his community without the burden of corporate overhead, inflated cost, or cutting corners.

A devoted husband of seven years and father to five wonderful children, Talon is a family man through and through. He is also a committed member of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, and a proud U.S. Army National Guard veteran, where he honed the discipline and sense of duty that continue to shape his approach to leadership and service.

"Being a mother is the hardest job on planet Earth."

The name Lioness Roofing comes from Talon's deep admiration for his wife - the heart of their home. Her daily selflessness, discipline, and unwavering love as a mother and matriarch inspired Talon to name the company in her honor. Through the name Lioness Roofing, he pays tribute to her strength, grace, and fierce dedication to their family.
