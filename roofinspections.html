Roof Inspections

Comprehensive HAAG-Style Inspections

At Lioness Roofing, our inspections go far beyond a simple walk-around. We perform a comprehensive 10-point HAAG-style inspection that examines both the exterior and the attic interior to uncover issues most contractors overlook. By getting inside your attic, we can evaluate ventilation, moisture intrusion, and structural integrity—ensuring we understand your roof from the inside out.

What sets us apart is our deep experience with insurance claims. Every inspection is conducted with the same precision and documentation standards required to stand up in an insurance review. We know exactly what carriers look for, and we make sure your inspection leaves no question about the condition or cause of damage.

Diagnosing Problems and Building Solutions

During our inspection, we identify how your roof can be properly brought up to code and beyond. We look for design flaws like dead valleys or roof-to-wall terminations that are prone to leaking and develop a clear game plan to correct them. Whether that means adding pan metal, rolled roofing tie-ins, or custom-fitted flashing, our goal is to build a system that performs flawlessly—no matter how the original roof was designed.

Because insurance restoration is one of our specialties, we also make sure every recommended repair or replacement is properly justified and documented for insurance adjusters. This ensures you get the full scope of work you’re entitled to—without unnecessary delays or denials.

Documentation and Proof That Protects You

We thoroughly photograph and video every inspection, documenting existing damage and potential problem areas. This not only protects homeowners but also provides verifiable, timestamped evidence for insurance claims. Every elevation—gutters, downspouts, windows, siding, fascia, and soffit—is inspected so we can notify you of any issues and offer real solutions before they grow into bigger problems.

Our reports are built to advocate for you, clearly showing the difference between normal wear and true storm-related damage. We bridge the gap between homeowner and insurer, ensuring your claim is supported by facts, not opinions.

Empirical Evidence. Ironclad Advocacy.

When we’re on your roof, we take our time—documenting shingle conditions, checking repairability, and verifying whether materials are discontinued or unavailable. If they are, we build strong, evidence-based cases with insurers grounded in irreparability, incompatibility, and unavailability.

We understand insurance language, carrier guidelines, and policy standards—and we use that knowledge to protect our clients and maximize their rightful coverage. We leave no stone unturned and no doubt unproven, ensuring every inspection stands up to scrutiny.

Our Promise

In summary, we inspect to uncover storm damage, builder errors, code violations, and hidden flaws, then craft a precise plan to correct them with proper materials and expert workmanship. And when it comes to insurance, we’re more than contractors—we’re advocates.

At Lioness Roofing, we stand beside you through the entire process, from inspection to final approval, making sure your roof—and your home—get the justice they deserve.
